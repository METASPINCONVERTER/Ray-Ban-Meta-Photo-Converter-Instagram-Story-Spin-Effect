General Questions

What is a Ray-Ban Meta photo converter?
A Ray-Ban Meta photo converter is a specialized image utility that reformats standard camera photos to match the exact hardware and metadata specifications of Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses. It resizes and cover-crops images to 3024 × 4032 pixels (3:4 portrait ratio), applies standard sRGB color profiling, and splices authentic Meta AI camera EXIF tags into the JPEG header so Instagram unlocks its native 3D Parallax Spin Story effect.
Do I need to own Ray-Ban Meta glasses to use this tool?
No. MetaSpin was built specifically so anyone can experience the 3D parallax spin view on Instagram Stories without owning the physical hardware. You can convert photos taken from iPhones, Androids, mirrorless cameras, or downloaded images.
Is this service completely free?
Yes, rayBEN is completely free with no subscriptions, limits, or hidden fees. We also never place watermarks or visual overlays on your converted photos.

Privacy & Security

Are my photos uploaded to any server or cloud?
No. The entire conversion, cropping, and EXIF injection happens locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and client-side binary manipulation. Your photo never leaves your device and is never sent to any external server.
What happens to GPS and location data in my photos?
rayBEN automatically strips the entire GPS IFD table from your photo's EXIF metadata. This ensures that personal location data (such as home or workplace coordinates) is completely removed before sharing on public social platforms.

Instagram Compatibility

Why does the image have to be exactly 3024 × 4032 pixels?
3024 × 4032 is the native sensor resolution of the 12 Megapixel camera in Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses. Instagram's feature-detection system checks both the EXIF hardware signature and this exact pixel geometry before activating the Spin View button.
Why isn't the 3D spin button showing up in my Instagram Story?
The three most common causes are:
  1. Sending the photo through messaging apps like WhatsApp, Telegram, or AirDrop with compression enabled, which strips EXIF tags.
  2. Posting to the regular feed carousel instead of Instagram Story.
  3. Using an outdated version of the Instagram app.
Can I post converted photos to regular Instagram Feed or Reels?
You can post the photos anywhere, but the 3D Parallax Spin view effect is an interactive feature exclusive to Instagram Stories.
