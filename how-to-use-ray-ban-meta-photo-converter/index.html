How to Convert Any Image to Ray-Ban Meta Format (Step-by-Step)

A simple, complete guide on transforming everyday smartphone photos into Instagram-ready smart-glasses media.

By MetaSpin Editorial Team • 4 min read • Updated March 2026

Prerequisites

You don't need expensive software or hardware. All you need is:

  • Any digital photo (JPEG, PNG, or WEBP).
  • A modern web browser (Safari on iOS, Chrome on Android or Desktop).
  • The latest version of the Instagram app installed on your phone.

Step 1: Open the MetaSpin Converter

Navigate to the rayBEN homepage. You have three ways to import your photo:

  • Take Photo: Tap to capture directly with your smartphone's camera.
  • Photo Library: Browse your phone's photo library or computer folders.
  • Paste (Ctrl+V): Copy an image to your clipboard and hit Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V on Mac) anywhere on the page.

Step 2: Automated Conversion & Metadata Injection

Once you select your image, rayBEN takes over automatically in fractions of a second:

  1. Corrects EXIF orientation so your photo is always right-side up.
  2. Crops and rescales the canvas to precisely 3024 × 4032 pixels (3:4 ratio).
  3. Removes sensitive GPS coordinates for privacy.
  4. Splices in authentic Make: Meta AI and Model: Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses 2 EXIF identifiers.

Step 3: Download or Share the Converted Photo

When processing completes, tap Share Image on mobile to send directly to Instagram, or tap Download High-Res JPEG to save the file to your device.

Important Tip: Camera Roll Integrity

Always import the image into Instagram directly from your phone's camera roll. Avoid emailing it through services that compress attachments or sending it through messaging apps (WhatsApp, WeChat, Telegram) that discard EXIF data.
