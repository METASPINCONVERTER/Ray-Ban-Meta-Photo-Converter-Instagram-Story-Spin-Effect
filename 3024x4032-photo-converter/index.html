3024×4032 Photo Converter for Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses

How to reformat images of any resolution or aspect ratio into the native 3:4 portrait resolution demanded by Meta smart-glasses features.

By MetaSpin Technical Team • 4 min read • Updated March 2026

Why 3024 × 4032 is the Magic Resolution

Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses feature an on-board sensor configured to shoot 12 Megapixels in a vertical orientation. In digital imaging mathematics, a 12 Megapixel 3:4 image corresponds to:

3024 (Width) × 4032 (Height) = 12,192,768 Pixels

When you upload an image to an Instagram Story, Instagram's media ingestion pipeline reads the raster dimensions. If the dimensions deviate (for example, 1080 × 1920 or 4032 × 3024), Instagram classifies the image as a mobile phone screenshot or landscape photo and skips the smart glasses parallax pipeline.

How MetaSpin's Cover-Crop Algorithm Works

When converting an image to 3024 × 4032, simple stretching or letterboxing would introduce black bars or distort facial proportions. MetaSpin utilizes an intelligent cover-crop geometry calculation:

  1. Aspect Ratio Evaluation: Compares the source image's aspect ratio against target 3 / 4 (0.75).
  2. Centered Crop Window: Computes the maximum visible central rectangle from the original photo without distorting pixel aspect ratio.
  3. High-Fidelity Canvas Rescaling: Employs bicubic image smoothing on an off-screen HTML5 Canvas configured with imageSmoothingQuality = 'high'.
  4. JPEG 92% Re-encoding: Outputs an optimal quality-to-filesize JPEG before injecting the Meta EXIF APP1 header.
Works with Landscape and Square Photos

Whether your original photo is a wide 16:9 panoramic shot, a classic 1:1 square, or a high-res mirrorless RAW export, MetaSpin centers the focal area and renders it directly to 3024 × 4032.
