Ray-Ban Meta Instagram Spin Effect Not Showing? How to Fix It

If you uploaded a photo to Instagram Story and the 3D spin view badge didn't appear, here are the exact causes and fixes.

By rayBEN Technical Team • 5 min read • Updated March 2026

Common Reason 1: The Photo was Sent via a Messaging App

This is by far the most frequent issue. Apps like WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook Messenger, iMessage (with Low Quality Mode enabled), or Discord compress photos aggressively to save bandwidth and systematically wipe all EXIF metadata.

The Solution

Download or transfer the photo directly to your phone using USB file transfer, AirDrop (with "All Photos Data" enabled), or save it directly in your mobile browser using rayBEN.

Common Reason 2: Uploading to Feed Instead of Instagram Story

The 3D parallax spin view is currently an Instagram Story-exclusive feature. If you try to post the photo as a regular Feed post or Reel, Instagram will display it as a standard static photo with no spin badge.

The Solution

Swipe right on your Instagram home feed to open the Story camera, tap the photo picker in the lower-left corner, and pick your photo there.

Common Reason 3: Photo was Cropped or Edited in Another App

If you opened your converted photo in VSCO, Lightroom Mobile, Snapseed, or Apple Photos and applied a crop or export preset, that editing app likely re-encoded the JPEG and replaced the Make: Meta AI metadata with its own software tag or altered the 3024 × 4032 pixel dimensions.

The Solution

Apply all creative color grading and filters before running the photo through rayBEN, so that rayBEN's 3024×4032 framing and Meta AI EXIF injection is the final step before posting.

Common Reason 4: Instagram App is Outdated

Meta AI smart-glasses detection is continuously updated in the official Instagram client for iOS and Android. Older app versions may fail to recognize recent hardware tag variants.

The Solution

Visit the Apple App Store or Google Play Store and update Instagram to the latest version.
